# The Killybegs Seafood Shack & Anderson Hospitality Group — Business Profile

> Single source of truth for website copy and metadata.

---

## 1. Core Business Overview & Identity

- **Business Name:** The Killybegs Seafood Shack (by Anderson Hospitality Group)
- **Founders / Key People:** Chef Garry Anderson & Mairéad Anderson
- **Location:** The Old Pier, Shore Road, Killybegs, County Donegal, Ireland (Wild Atlantic Way)
- **Business Model:** Pier-side gourmet seafood takeaway (boat-to-fryer) operated out of an authentic nautical trailer right on Ireland's premier commercial deepwater fishing harbor.

### Sister Entities

- **Anderson's Boathouse Restaurant:** Full sit-down dining venue on Main Street overlooking the harbor.
- **Killybegs Luxury Harbour Apartments:** Boutique self-catering accommodations for tourists visiting Slieve League and the Wild Atlantic Way.

---

## 2. Key Culinary Pedigree & Accolades

- **Head Chef Experience:** Garry Anderson trained under Gordon Ramsay at Claridge's London and formerly served as Head Chef at the 5-star Lough Eske Castle.

### Major Awards

- **All-Ireland Chowder Champion:** Winner (2019 & 2020)
- **Donegal's Best Chowder:** Winner (2018)
- **Irish Restaurant Awards:** Ulster's Best Newcomer (2019 — Anderson's Boathouse)
- **TripAdvisor:** Ranked #1 Quick Bite in Donegal with over 1,000+ top reviews
- **National Press:** Featured in *The Irish Times*, *The Irish Independent*, and *RTE Food*.

## 3. Core Menu Items & Descriptions (For Your Bento Grid)

### Signature Catch Box (Cod & Chips)
> Fresh Atlantic wild cod in a light, golden-amber crispy beer batter served over steaming hand-cut sea salt chips with homemade tartare sauce and lemon.

### Award-Winning Seafood Chowder
> Rich, creamy broth loaded with fresh smoked haddock, wild salmon chunks, and Donegal blue mussels, served with traditional homemade Irish soda bread.

### Crispy Scampi Prawns
> Jumbo Atlantic scampi prawns coated in seasoned, crunchy breadcrumbs and fried to golden perfection.

### Salt & Chili Calamari
> Tender calamari rings tossed in sea salt, fresh chili flakes, and lime zest, served with house garlic mayonnaise.

### Donegal Garlic Crab Claws
> Locally caught Atlantic crab claws pan-fried in rich herb-and-garlic butter.
